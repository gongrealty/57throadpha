Why Penthouse A

What sets it apart

01Protected skyline views

Open outlooks by day and glittering skyline by night — reaching the Freedom Tower to the west and Citi Field to the east. Surrounded by low-rise houses and zoning that keeps it that way, the views are here to stay.

02Private outdoor space

A generous private terrace plus balconies extend the home outdoors — room to dine, entertain, and take in the fireworks on the Fourth of July.

03Duplex & house-like

Two full levels connected by a spiral staircase live like a townhouse in the sky — bedrooms and living separated across floors for real privacy.

04A great room to gather

A nearly 400-square-foot living and dining great room with hardwood floors and walls of windows — an unusually large, light-filled space for the neighborhood.

05Solid-wood kitchen

Real solid-oak cabinetry, granite counters, and gas cooking — open to the living space and framed by the spiral stair.

06Power that never quits

The building shares an electrical grid feed with the neighboring former hospital — so the lights stay on through storms and outages that darken the rest of the block. Backed by a live-in superintendent, elevator, and dedicated rooftop mechanicals.
